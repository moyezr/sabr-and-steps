# Product brief

## Confirmed requirements

- Channel: **Sabr & Steps**.
- English-only Islamic reassurance and practical self-help for people experiencing hardship.
- Regular Qur'an translations and hadith with attribution and source references.
- Normal duration 1–3 minutes; maximum 5 minutes.
- Landscape videos and vertical Shorts, with captions at the visual center.
- Creator-uploaded still images or animated GIF backgrounds, configurable background audio, and optional AI narration. Video backgrounds are deferred.
- Text-only reading cards with background music or silence, adjustable reading speed, image framing/darkness, and audio level/loop/fades.
- Voice, gender, pace, and tone remain configurable while the creator auditions them.
- Goal of 2–3 videos per week, a low initial budget, and optional paid credits.
- Flexible creator oversight: individual stages or a consolidated final review.
- Local app, database, rendering, and media storage; OpenRouter LLMs and direct Cartesia/ElevenLabs/Deepgram speech APIs.

## Pilot workflow

1. Enter an idea, intended emotional need, and approximate duration.
2. Inspect canonical sources and surrounding context.
3. Generate and edit a script with separately identified quotations and reflections.
4. Upload/select a background and track. Choose narration with aligned captions, or text-only reading cards without a voice call.
5. Save a preview revision, inspect both aspect ratios, edit the words or picture/sound, and save again. Previous exports remain accessible.
6. Render MP4s, captions, and a description with references for manual upload.

The accepted direction is a connected episode editor with Idea, Script & sources, Voice & captions, Music, Backgrounds, Video, and Exports. Each section remains reachable throughout creation; unavailable actions explain their prerequisites. The shared episode identity and stage states help the creator move between existing words, media, preview, and downloads without restarting the workflow.

Milestone 1 is delivered as F008: the existing editing routes now reopen the most developed available work, show accurate saved, stale, generating, failed, and uncertain states, and guard unsaved edits on navigation. Existing uploads, caption timing, preview revisions, and download history are reused. New episodes show useful empty states in every section.

F009 delivers the script-first part of milestone 2: revision-checked autosaved working drafts, named immutable checkpoints, explicit persisted script selection, history restore-as-new, and fresh alternatives that do not replace selected work. Downstream readiness follows the selected script. Undo/redo, side-by-side comparison, explicit voice/caption/composition selectors, AI partial rewrites, a scene timeline, richer sound/visual controls, and format-specific exports remain planned.

## Visual and editorial direction

Start with a calm nature background or restrained gradient, high-contrast centered text, short phrase groups, gentle transitions, and a readable source line. HeroUI supplies studio controls; Remotion supplies deterministic video animation.

Use a shared composition with 16:9 and 9:16 layouts. Preview mobile readability and platform overlays before fixing exact font sizes or margins. The five-minute maximum applies to the content system; eligibility as a YouTube Short depends on current platform rules and must be checked during export implementation.

The proposed editorial standard is hopeful, source-grounded encouragement with one achievable practical step. Hooks should describe a situation without claiming the algorithm is a divine message or promising a dated outcome. See [content guidance](content.md).

## First outcome

Complete one original 90–120 second episode with a verified Qur'an reference, narration, synchronized captions, and two playable exports. Prove this workflow before adding scheduling or broad automation. Hadith ingestion follows as a required channel capability in the feature tracker.

| Background sound | Configurable none, nature ambience, or sourced audio; creator chooses the Islamic audio approach |
| Weekly count | Assumption: 2–3 original episodes, each with two exports; confirm before scheduling work |
| First topic | Choose during the pilot; waiting/perseverance are possible starting themes |
